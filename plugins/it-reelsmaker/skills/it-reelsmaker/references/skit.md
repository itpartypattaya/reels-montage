# Two-person skit / “Verdict”

Graphics and camera for a dialogue between two people with an expert conclusion. Read it when the brief is a skit.

> A library, not a template: each element only per the brief; if the video is not about an assessment, don't offer the scale.

- **the hook is the mechanics**: roles and scale visible from the first frame, the first line is the most controversial one (from later in the video as a teaser if needed: `structure.md`); with the scale on screen from the start the video becomes a game: the viewer guesses the verdict and stays to check it;
- **role tags** above the heads for the whole video: the “Marker” variant is a light card with dark all-caps text, ~190 px above the head, following the virtual camera, hidden while thesis cards are on screen; the “Glass” variant is `{{DARK}}` at 35–45 %, blur 12–16 px, a white outline at ~25 % and an `{{ACCENT}}` glow at ~40 % along the bottom edge;
- **verdict scale** ⟨YOURS: your own three values⟩ at waist level; the mark: the word moves onto a `{{MARKER}}` marker bar, or the focus brackets close in on the word. The mark is drawn as a stroke over 0.3–0.5 s and held 1–2 s; when the verdict is the last line before the logo sting or the end card, there is no room for 1–2 s after it: start the mark on the verdict's first word and let it fade out with the sting. A red circle / green check mark only if the brand allows it (it is a second bright color);
- **subtitles** colored per speaker: the speakers go into `cut.json` (`"speaker": "A"` on each range, `"speakers": [{"from": 120.4, "to": 121.9, "speaker": "B"}]` for a switch inside a range, source seconds; one microphone: identified by the lips, a diarizer mixes the two up), `cut.py` puts the label on every caption word and the kit colors them in every mode: the first label (sorted) in the mode's own colors, the second in the accent; “Bar” changes the plate, “Typewriter” and “Accent” the text. Other colors: `brand.json → colors.speakers` (a list in that order; “Bar” keeps the style's marker for the first speaker); check a light speaker color over the darkening with `visual_plan.py shade --color`;
- **“save”**: bookmark icon + hand cursor, 110–130 px, on a useful line in the second half of the video, 1–1.5 s, twice at most, not a second CTA;
- **camera**: wide ×1.0, medium ×1.2; a medium shot shifted toward one person (~×1.25) is fine, a close-up on one person is not (it cuts the other face); the hook is a wide shot without a push-in, so both people and the mechanics are visible (the skit's exception to the general “the first 1–2 s are not a static wide shot” of `camera.md`: here the hook is the line and the mechanics on screen); a punch-in up to ~×1.3, centered, over 6–8 frames, back after 1.5–3 s, no more than once every 10 s.

Layout 1080×1920: UI 0–220 · roles 240–360 · faces ~400–800 · scale ~880–1040 · mark ~1040–1220 · subtitles 1250–1430 (the kit's subtitle band; a per-video composition that needs them elsewhere moves them with `subtitlesTop`) · UI 1500–1920 (refine by frames). Shooting: both people at the same height, 15–20% free background above their heads, each with their own lavalier mic.
